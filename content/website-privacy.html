2. Visits to this website

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Delivering a page involves processing network and request information, such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time. GitHub handles hosting information under its privacy statement.

We do not add website analytics, advertising trackers, third-party fonts, embedded social widgets, or cookies for marketing. The site’s scripts do not create persistent browser-storage identifiers.

3. Party invitations

A Piccolo Squad invitation URL contains the party code, region, game version, room identifier, and sometimes a cluster. The party page reads these fields in your browser to show the invitation and prepare an Android open-game link. It does not send a separate request to a game backend or an analytics service.

The URL is still sent to the hosting provider when the page loads and can appear in browser history or in messages where you share it. Treat the complete link as an invitation, share it carefully, and ask the host for a fresh one when needed. The Copy link button writes the invitation to your clipboard only when you choose it.

4. Email and external links

Contact and deletion-request buttons open your own email app with a subject or draft message. This website does not submit a form or send an email by itself. If you send the message, we and our email providers process your address and the information you include to handle your request.

We keep support correspondence only as needed for the request, follow-up matters, and legal obligations. External provider links are labeled; their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.
