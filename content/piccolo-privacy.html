This policy covers Piccolo Squad on Android. The game uses online services for multiplayer and social features. Advertising and optional analytics have separate purposes. For the other game, read the Brainrot Bills Android policy.

1. Who is responsible

Radi Akka, an individual game developer based in Morocco, publishes this game under Khoroto Global and is responsible for the processing described here. Contact: support@khorotoglobal.xyz.

2. Information used by the game

  • On your device: progress, earned virtual items and balances, settings, character and name choices, privacy choices, and credentials used to resume an online session.
  • Online identity and social features: a pseudonymous Unity player identifier, public player code, display name and character information, friends and blocked-player relationships, social preferences, and account/service notices. A guest is an online account even when no email or password is requested.
  • Multiplayer and invitations: network connection information, nickname, room and region identifiers, game version, party state, short-lived presence and invitation records, and data needed to synchronize co-op play.
  • Leaderboards: player identifiers, displayed names, scores, and associated game or ranking information when you use online rankings. Other players can see information displayed on a leaderboard.
  • Optional phone notifications: installation/device identifiers, a push-delivery token, notification preferences, and invitation or friend-activity messages when you enable the feature.
  • Optional analytics and diagnostics: technical device and app information, gameplay events, session context, and limited scrubbed error reports after the in-game consent choice permits collection.
  • Support and safety: your email address and whatever you send us; in-game reports contain the reporter and reported player identifiers, a report category, and a timestamp.

We do not ask for your real name to play, your contacts, precise GPS location, microphone recordings, or camera access. Please avoid personal information in your multiplayer name or reports.

3. Why information is processed

We use this information to provide the features you request, connect the right players, maintain accounts and rankings, deliver selected notifications, handle reports, prevent abuse, diagnose problems, and answer requests. Optional analytics helps us understand gameplay and improve the game.

Where applicable, we rely on consent for optional analytics and personalized advertising; performance of the requested service for accounts, multiplayer, and social features; legitimate interests for security, support, and service reliability; and legal obligations when required. A provider may need consent for device access or other processing even when ads are contextual.

4. Advertising and choices

The Android game is supported by advertising, including banners in supported menus, interstitials at transition points, and optional rewarded ads. It does not offer real-money purchases or subscriptions in this Android version.

Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads provide advertising. They may process advertising or installation identifiers, IP address and approximate location, technical device information, ad interactions, and consent signals to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. See their official information in our advertising service directory.

Declining or withdrawing the in-game data choice requests non-personalized ads and stops optional Unity Analytics collection. Contextual ads still involve technical processing. Change the in-game choice under Settings → ACCOUNT & PRIVACY → Data Privacy. Android’s advertising settings also let you reset or delete the advertising ID, depending on your OS version.

We do not trade personal data for money. Personalized advertising can qualify as a “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising under some US state laws. Decline personalization, use the available ad-provider privacy controls, or contact us to exercise applicable rights.

5. Who receives information

Photon carries multiplayer traffic. Unity Authentication, Friends, and Leaderboards provide online identity and social/ranking services; Unity Analytics handles consented analytics. Our social backend runs on Cloudflare Workers and D1. Firebase Cloud Messaging supports Android phone notifications. Advertising providers receive the data described above. Email providers process support correspondence.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect people or the service, or to providers helping us perform these functions. Provider roles and practices vary by service; their official policies are linked in the service directory.

6. What other people can see

Players you interact with can see your displayed name, character, and relevant party or ranking information. Friends may see activity or receive invitations according to your social choices. Anyone with a party URL can read its room code, region, and connection details, so share invitations carefully. A valid link does not guarantee that a room is still open.

7. Your controls

  • Change optional data consent using Data Privacy in Settings. This preserves the online account; consent withdrawal and account deletion are different actions.
  • Manage friends, blocks, invitations, and notification preferences in the game. You can also disable phone notifications in Android Settings.
  • Request account deletion in Settings → ACCOUNT & PRIVACY → Delete Account, or use our web deletion-request instructions.
  • Clearing app storage affects local saves and credentials. It does not itself submit a request to remove online records.

8. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until replaced or removed from your device. Online identity, friends, profiles, public player codes, and preferences are kept while needed to provide the account and requested features, unless you delete them or applicable law requires otherwise.

Party tickets, presence, and invitation deliveries expire after short operational periods. Expiration makes them unusable; physical cleanup occurs through service cleanup operations. Report cleanup is configured for records older than 30 days when new reports are processed. Account deletion removes associated social records and requests removal of the Unity account and leaderboard entries. A short-lived hashed account marker prevents an unexpired access token from recreating deleted social data.

Providers keep their own operational, advertising, analytics, and backup records under their policies. We keep support correspondence only as needed to answer the request, resolve follow-up issues, and meet legal obligations. Limited records may need to remain for security, disputes, or law; we will explain relevant limits when handling a request.

9. Privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of data; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of targeted advertising, sale, or sharing. This includes applicable rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR, US state privacy laws, and Morocco’s Law No. 09-08. You may also complain to the relevant data-protection authority, including Morocco’s CNDP.

Email support@khorotoglobal.xyz with the game name and request. We may ask for proportionate proof of account ownership and respond within the period required by applicable law. Do not send passwords or access tokens. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable rights.

10. Children

Piccolo Squad is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child supplied personal information should contact us so we can review the situation and remove applicable data. The minimum age for independently giving valid consent can be higher in some places.

11. Security and international processing

We use safeguards appropriate to the service, including encrypted service connections and access controls. No system guarantees complete security. Providers operate internationally, so information may be processed outside Morocco or your country, subject to applicable transfer requirements and provider safeguards.

12. Changes and contact

We update this page when practices change and revise the date above. Material changes may also be communicated in the game or through another appropriate notice.
