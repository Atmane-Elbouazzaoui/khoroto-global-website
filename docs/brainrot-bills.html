Smash eggs for cash, settle the bills, and keep the workshop going. Fill your collection and take on Overtime when you’re ready for more pressure.

Earn the extras.

The Android version is supported by ads. Optional rewarded ads let you earn unlocks and redraws. There are no real-money purchases or subscriptions in this Android version.

An internet connection is required to keep playing after the initial runs. Progress is saved locally; there is no game account or cloud-save service.
