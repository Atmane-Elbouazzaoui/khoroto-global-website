Engine and game services

The games are built with Unity. Piccolo Squad also uses Photon PUN and Unity Gaming Services; its optional Android notifications use Firebase Cloud Messaging. Advertising uses Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads. These services and their marks belong to their respective owners.
