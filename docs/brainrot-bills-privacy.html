This policy covers Brainrot Bills: Smash an Egg on Android. Progress is saved locally, but the game uses internet services for advertising and requires a connection to keep playing after the initial runs. For Piccolo Squad, use its separate Android privacy policy.

1. Developer and scope

Radi Akka is an individual game developer based in Morocco, publishing under Khoroto Global, and is responsible for the processing described here. Email: armando.b.higgin@gmail.com.

This policy applies to this Android game. It does not automatically cover other games or versions with different services.

2. Local game data

The game stores progress, earned content, settings, advertising choices, and ad-frequency or reward-unlock records on your device. It has no game accounts, cloud saves, developer-operated gameplay servers, real-money purchases, or subscriptions in this Android version.

We do not operate a server that receives your local save. The game does not integrate a separate gameplay analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting service. Advertising SDKs can still process operational and diagnostic information as described below. Device or operating-system backup features may copy local app data according to your own backup settings.

3. Advertising services

The Android game uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads for interstitial ads between runs and optional rewarded ads that unlock content or provide redraws. Rewarded ads require your choice to watch; an unavailable or unfinished ad does not earn the reward.

Advertising providers may process advertising or installation identifiers, IP address and approximate location, device and app information, ad requests and interactions, diagnostics, and consent signals. The purposes include ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and personalization where permitted. Their official privacy information is linked in our service directory.

Local saves do not make the game free of data processing: ad requests are network requests to advertising providers.

4. Your advertising choice

Before advertising starts, the game asks whether to request personalized ads. You can change that decision in VIBE CHECK → PRIVACY CHOICES, which opens the AD PRIVACY screen.

Declining personalization requests basic/contextual ads. It does not turn off every ad or the technical processing needed to deliver and measure them. The game remains playable with either advertising choice, subject to its internet requirement. Changing your choice affects future requests and does not automatically erase data already held by providers.

Android’s advertising settings can also let you reset or delete the advertising ID, depending on your OS version. Ad-provider privacy controls may offer additional choices.

5. Support information and permissions

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. We use these to answer questions, investigate problems, handle ad or privacy reports, and keep a record of the request.

The game does not request your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location. The Android system share sheet or your mail app handles the content you choose to share or send. Please remove unnecessary personal information from screenshots.

6. Sharing and legal bases

Information is processed by the advertising providers above and their relevant service or advertising partners, and by email providers when you contact us. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect rights or safety, or to resolve an abuse or security issue.

Where applicable, we rely on consent for personalized advertising and device access that requires consent; legitimate interests for support, security, and service operation where lawful; and legal obligations when required. Contextual advertising may still require consent for particular device access or processing under local law.

We do not trade personal information for money. Personalized advertising can qualify as a “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising under some US state laws. You can decline personalization, use provider privacy controls, or contact us about applicable opt-out rights.

7. Retention

Local game records remain until the app replaces them or you remove the app’s stored data. Reinstalling may restore backed-up data depending on your device settings. Advertising providers retain their records under their own policies; their retention is not controlled by the local save file.

We keep support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the request, follow-up issues, and legal obligations. When information must be retained for a legal, security, or dispute reason, we limit its use to that purpose.

8. Deletion and privacy rights

To remove local progress, use Android’s app-storage controls to clear Brainrot Bills data. This resets the game and earned unlocks on the device. Uninstalling or clearing storage does not submit a deletion request to advertising providers.

There is no Brainrot Bills game account to delete. For support records or other data we hold, email armando.b.higgin@gmail.com. For provider-held advertising data, use the provider’s privacy tools; we can help identify the relevant provider. Do not email passwords, private tokens, or unnecessary device identifiers.

Depending on your location, you may have rights of access, correction, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, and opt-out of targeted advertising, sale, or sharing. These include applicable rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR, US state privacy laws, and Morocco’s Law No. 09-08. We respond within the period required by applicable law and may request proportionate verification. You may complain to your relevant data-protection authority, including Morocco’s CNDP.

9. Children

The game is intended for players 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has supplied information, contact us so we can investigate and arrange removal of applicable data. The age for independently giving valid consent may be higher in your location.

10. International processing and security

Advertising and email providers operate internationally. Information may be processed outside Morocco or your country, subject to applicable transfer requirements and provider safeguards. We use reasonable safeguards for information we handle, but no system is completely secure.

11. Changes and contact

We update the date above when this policy changes and provide additional notice when appropriate for a material change.
