Last reviewed: November 1, 2026

This directory identifies the services used by the Android games and the website. The Piccolo Squad policy and Brainrot Bills policy explain when each category is used. Links below lead to the providers’ official websites.

Advertising — both games

Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and Unity Ads provide mediation and advertising. Their policies explain advertising data and privacy controls, including relevant advertising partners.

Unity game-player and app-user privacy policy · ironSource privacy information

Piccolo Squad online services

  • Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Friends, Leaderboards, and optional Analytics. Unity player privacy policy.
  • Photon / Exit Games: real-time multiplayer networking. Photon privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Workers and D1: hosting and storage for the social backend. Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: optional Android phone-notification delivery. Firebase privacy and security information.

Website hosting and contact

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub privacy statement.

Support email sent to the published Gmail address is processed by email services. Google privacy policy. You choose whether to send a message through your own email provider.

Requests and choices

Use in-game controls for future advertising choices and optional notifications. For data requests, start with account and data deletion or contact Radi Akka. Some provider-held records need a separate request through the provider’s tools.
